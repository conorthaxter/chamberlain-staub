A road trip, listening tour and living archive of conversations with musicians known and unknown. We hit the road for a month in May 2026 and filmed over 88 conversations along with performances and community gatherings across the country. We kept the crew small — only four people, moving light and fast, relying on natural lighting and clip-on microphones. The result is a series of conversations that feel more like riding shotgun.
